# Credits

## The character

**Capri** (Project Capricorn) is a character from *Cyberpunk: Edgerunners*. Cyberpunk, Cyberpunk: Edgerunners and
their characters belong to CD PROJEKT RED, Studio Trigger and Netflix. Capricorn is an unofficial, noncommercial fan
project, not affiliated with or endorsed by any of them.

Her artwork in this repository (`art/` and her rig in `ui/public/rig/`) is not covered by the MIT license of the code.
If a rights holder wants it removed, open an issue.

## Her look

The cut-out of her that her rig is traced from (`art/reference/capri-cutout.png`) comes from
[mochiiwuff/capri-desktop](https://github.com/mochiiwuff/capri-desktop). Capricorn traces it into its own vector rig,
poses and animation (`art/trace/`).

## Earlier Capri companions

Capri on the desktop started with [mochiiwuff/capri-desktop](https://github.com/mochiiwuff/capri-desktop) and
[lachydotmcg/capri-desktop](https://github.com/lachydotmcg/capri-desktop), and a few of her habits here are a nod to
them. Capricorn is a separate implementation, written from scratch in Rust and TypeScript, and shares no code with
either.
